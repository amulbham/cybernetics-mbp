## Content-shaped (grow the corpus, these activate on their own)

- **Related-research module** (`RelatedResearch.astro`) is fully built and correct, but won't visibly show anything until at least two entries share a pillar (papers only) or a tag — right now there are 2 papers (different pillars) and 1 essay with zero tag overlap between any of them, so it's still empty everywhere by construction. Nothing to build; it activates naturally as more content gets published.
- **Tag governance**: a hard `z.enum()` on tags was considered and rejected as disproportionate for a solo-author site (see `CHANGELOG.md` 2026-08-14). A casing rule *was* decided 2026-08-24 (sentence case, with a paper's own coined/named terms excepted as proper nouns — documented in `AGENTS.md`) and enforced by the content-manager skill going forward; what's still open is a thin-content generation threshold for `/tags/[tag]/` pages, which would currently delete every existing tag page (each has a count of exactly 1). Revisit once there's actual tag overlap to threshold against — check current counts before picking a number, the way the TOC threshold was picked from real data rather than guessed.
- **Two of five pillars have a published paper** — `policy-systems` (the FAFSA paper) and `cybernetics` (the Invariants paper). `ai-systems`, `seo-architecture`, and `systems-architecture` currently have zero — confirmed `research/[category]/index.astro` and `research/index.astro` both handle this gracefully for pillars (no page/section generated for a pillar with no papers, not an empty broken section — `essays`/`memos` are the deliberate exception, see `AGENTS.md` → "Categories/URLs"; the homepage pillar grid is a further deliberate exception on top of that, showing all 5 unconditionally as navigation). Fills in naturally as real papers get added to those pillars.
