## A. Identity and scholarly page-1 hierarchy — intended future output

- Author name: **Amul Bham**, exact normal mixed case, visually and extractably intact on page 1. No CSS synthetic small caps or one-glyph-per-token spacing for the byline. Do not add affiliation, degrees, pseudonyms or journal status to the `citation_author` field.
- Public profession: **Systems Researcher** as the short display role, once T28.2 resolves role vs institutional affiliation and owners approve exact copy. **It is not the name of an institution.** Do not populate `affiliation` with this profession or invent a university/employer. If the manuscript actually needs an affiliation, express the truthful independent/no-institution status separately and only when required.
- Longer About the Author: **“Amul Bham is a systems researcher and AI systems architect.”** is the preferred *opening* at present, but final copy requires the T28.2 owner seal; retain the rest of the research bio. The shared `AUTHOR.bio` / `AuthorNote.astro` supplies both HTML and future generated PDFs, so avoid a second PDF-only biography string.
- Page 1 should make **paper title → actual author** unambiguous. Proposed Scholar-oriented fallback targets: title **≥24 pt**, author **16–23 pt** in normal case, with the actual author directly beneath the main title if possible. Preserve legitimate author-authored subtitles, but assess whether a subtitle before the author harms bibliographic recognition; choose ordering via a human-approved visual proof. These are design goals derived from Scholar's *layout fallback* guidance, **not** a guaranteed indexing/rich-result criterion.
- Preserve ORCID and correspondence visibility and legibility; preserve the distinct roles of paper author, contact location and institutional affiliation. Revision/publication dates and DOI must be accurate to approved source, not generated from current build time.

## B. Extraction and archival-quality standards for each new PDF
- Title, full author name, at least two sampled body phrases, references and published date **searchable and extracted correctly** using two independent extractors (e.g. pdf.js text and Poppler `pdftotext`). Specifically assert the whole **“Amul Bham”** appears cleanly adjacent to the title and no `A BHAM`/space-split author tokens occur in first-page extraction.
- Maintain fully crawlable PDF beside one canonical HTML scholarly article, paper-only Highwire metadata and accessible links. Check PDF size against current official Google Scholar guidance, HTML/full text/abstract, exact `citation_title`, author, original publication date, optional approved concept or version DOI, `citation_pdf_url` and JSON-LD identity. A schema/CI PASS is not evidence of actual Scholar indexing.
- Preserve text accessibility across paragraphs, unicode/math, line-end hyphenation and reference lists; test search on selected hyphenated terms. Type 3 font output was observed in all six historical PDFs, but extractable ToUnicode maps passed: investigate whether a feasible font/render option improves text extraction **without** changing unrelated paper typography. **No renderer/toolchain replacement merely to eliminate Type 3** unless separately justified and authorized.
- Keep figures truly embedded (not CSS placeholder), correct captions, reference links, non-clipped wide tables, section and page-break behavior, and running headers separate from human-readable references when feasible. Full desktop/print page contact sheet and spot-check first page, tables, Figure and references required.
- Keep `AuthorNote` in PDF, with navigational About link excluded; correct ORCID, license and declarations. Do not invent abstracts/declarations/citation links not present in authored paper. A scholarly article that is full-text HTML needs no artificial abstract simply for Scholar.
