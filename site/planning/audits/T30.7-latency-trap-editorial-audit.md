### What the examples do

The module is a `## Key findings` heading followed by a list; the build wraps both in `<section class="key-findings">` (callout box) and the heading appears in the TOC. Nothing else is needed.

### Recommended: 4 bullets, after the three opening paragraphs, before §1

Written to the essay's own hedging. Each carries the source location so the owner can check fidelity.

1. **A pause or a "thinking" indicator can suggest that effort was spent without showing what the effort was for, so a wait leaves open both whether enough work happened and whether the right work happened.**
   Source: opening ¶2; end of §1.
2. **In the one latency experiment discussed (240 participants), longer response-onset delays raised ratings of thoughtfulness, and a 9-second delay raised usefulness over 2 seconds, but task understanding showed no significant latency effect. Effort and understanding remain different judgments.**
   Source: §2, Tan et al. paragraphs.
3. **Interruption research suggests a possible recovery cost when a wait pulls attention elsewhere: short laboratory interruptions increased sequence errors and resuming involved incremental recovery of context. The findings do not establish a universal AI threshold.**
   Source: §3.
4. **The essay proposes a paired session-intent and turn-intent statement at the start of a response, giving users a specific interpretation to compare with their request without disclosing internal reasoning. It is a design proposal from one personal observation, not a demonstrated result, and it must stay open to comparison with execution.**
   Source: opening ¶3; §4; §6.

(In the page, bullets are plain text; bold above is only for this audit.)

Fidelity notes:
- Every number and hedge in bullets 2 and 3 is in the article (240 participants; 2/9/20 seconds; "no significant latency effect on it"; "not a universal AI threshold").
- Bullet 4 deliberately includes the "personal observation, not a controlled finding" caveat so the module does not read as stronger than the essay.
- Optional 5th bullet (not recommended, to match the 4-bullet precedent): the proposed test, "specific turn/session statements against generic acknowledgment or none, with delay varied".

**Open point:** this is new authored text, not moved text. The owner should edit the wording freely; I would not publish it unreviewed.
