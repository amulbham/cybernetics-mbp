## Current architectural evidence (source audit, NOT full external compliance PASS)
- `site/src/content.config.ts`: **frontmatter `format`** (`paper`/`essay`/`memo`) is the semantic class; `category` only routes the article. `site/src/layouts/ResearchLayout.astro` maps paper to `['ScholarlyArticle','Article']` plus Highwire (`citation_title`, `citation_author`, `citation_publication_date`, `citation_pdf_url`, optional `citation_doi`) and a generated PDF. Essay and memo map to plain `Article` without Scholar tags.
- `site/src/lib/structured-data.ts`: canonical Person `@id`/URL/sameAs used by homepage, About and research; `site/src/consts.ts` maintains site identity, author affiliation/bio and links. `site/src/pages/about.astro` currently sets `dateModified: new Date().toISOString()` on every build — evaluate whether that inaccurately declares a real content revision. It also hardcodes `jobTitle: 'Systems & AI Researcher'`, inconsistent with the requested new standardized role.
- `site/scripts/validate-research-semantics.mjs` independently reconstructs *relation-projection expectations* and checks emitted JSON-LD, Person and publisher, DOI/Highwire parity, canonical routing, audience and forbidden properties. But it imports production `personIdentity()` for expected Person state: external-quality/source-of-truth checks must also independently validate that these fields are **factually correct**, not merely internally identical.
- `site/scripts/validate-research-pdfs.mjs` verifies PDF existence/size/metadata link/Scholar identity, with visual checks still human-controlled. Code validators and Actions #134 PASS are **internal-contract evidence**, not equivalent to Googlebot/Scholar crawler inclusion or Google rich-result eligibility.
- Real article controls: `site/src/content/research/the-lab-bubble/index.md` **`format: essay`** → ordinary `Article`; `site/src/content/research/cognition-as-self-organizing-system/index.md` **`format: paper`** → ScholarlyArticle/Highwire, concept DOI `23068820`, original pubDate Sept 30 and approved Nov 2 dateModified. Their live pages require **separate independent external extraction**: prior web fetch to `amulbham.com` was unavailable in the Planner runtime; do not claim live external rich-result validation happened.
- Important potential areas to test: whether generated article OG image is representative and crawlable under Google's Article guidance; About page changing `dateModified` each build; consistency of `Person.sameAs` after Substack retirement; `ScholarlyArticle+Article` acceptance in Google Rich Results Test vs valid Schema.org; Scholar full/visible author-written abstracts, matching dates, PDF content and no false journal/venue tags.
- Primary external sources: [Google Scholar Inclusion Guidelines](https://scholar.google.com/intl/en/scholar/inclusion.html); [Google Article structured-data recommendations](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article); [general structured-data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies); [structured-data testing](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data).
