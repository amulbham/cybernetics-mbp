The question started simply: why does an LLM lose the thread? Not randomly — structurally. The failures clustered in consistent ways, and consistent failures have consistent causes.

I spent a decade in enterprise SEO, most recently as Principal SEO Manager at Skechers, building and integrating AI systems into business processes at scale. I knew what it looked like when a tool failed for surface reasons and when it failed for structural ones.

What I found underneath the memory problem wasn't a missing feature. It was a missing architecture. Building it — what became the Mission Brief Protocol and the Cognitive Physics Series — led somewhere I didn't plan: a set of invariant conditions that converge with work in cybernetics dating to the 1950s. I didn't build on that literature. I found the same structure from a different angle. That form of convergence is part of the evidence. I left corporate work in early 2026 to research full time.
