## Goal

Replace `pillar` with a required, centrally defined `category` as the sole routing input; separate editorial category from scholarly-PDF eligibility; migrate the five live entries; and make every retired public URL a direct 301 while all four scholarly PDFs survive.

## Implementation contract

- **Source of truth**: `category` (frontmatter) → `categorySegment()`; everything else derives. `format` alone decides PDF/Highwire/`ScholarlyArticle`/`isPartOf` (logic and call sites unchanged).
- **Fail closed**: a missing or unknown category, a retained `pillar`, `doi` on a non-paper, or a route-history violation fails the build or validator; no `undefined` route is ever emitted.
- **Route-history validation** (`validate:pdfs`, against generated artifacts):
  1. Every row's entry exists.
  2. The entry's current category (from frontmatter, via the shared helper) is in `row.categories`, so a published entry cannot move without an explicit append.
  3. `row.pdf` ⇒ the entry is `format: paper` and `dist` holds `{currentRoute}{id}.pdf`.
  4. `row.iwl` ⇒ the `iwl` collection holds a published IWL whose parent is the entry and `dist` holds the current `iwlPath()` page.
  5. Every generated redirect target exists in `dist`.
  6. No file exists at any non-current historical route or retired-category route (a retired path must be a redirect, never a shadow).
- **Redirect coverage**: for each historical category other than the entry's current one, emit the Article (with and without trailing slash), IWL when promised (both slash forms), canonical `{id}.pdf` and legacy `paper.pdf` when promised, and OG image. **For all four current `format: paper` routes, including Chisel under Knowledge Work and FAFSA under Essays, emit the current-route `paper.pdf` → current `{id}.pdf` 301** (T18.1, unchanged publication contract). Each retired category emits both slash forms. Every source maps directly to the final current URL in one hop; no PDF redirect chains or missing current-route legacy redirects.
- **Matrix handling and independent oracle**: the matrix below is the Product Authority-approved drafting baseline, not generated from `route-history.ts`. Before implementation, probe production and record observed 200/301/404 **separately** in the evidence file; freeze a checked, independent expected-source/target matrix derived from this approved contract. Preserve the approved retired-category 301 aliases (`seo-architecture`, `systems-architecture`) even if currently 404, plus approved no-slash variants: a 404 today is neither proof a URL was never published nor permission to discard an approved redirect. The generator and its validator may share `route-history.ts`, but acceptance **also compares their output to this independently frozen matrix** (a separate test oracle, not a second runtime category registry). An unexpected historically live source must be reported for inclusion; any proposed removal or material destination change, even for a 404, requires Product Authority approval before modifying the matrix. Record the verified final set/count; do not hard-code the count as an acceptance substitute.
- **Compatibility**: each current paper route continues to serve the T18.1 legacy `paper.pdf` redirect, including the **new** Knowledge Work/Essays paper locations. The sitemap lists current routes only; moved old routes remain redirect-only.
- **Rollback**: a revert commit on the deploying branch; Actions rebuilds the previous route set and `_redirects`.
