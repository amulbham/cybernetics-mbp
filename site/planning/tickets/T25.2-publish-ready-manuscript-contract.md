## Adversarial cases and regression boundaries

- **Zero-primitives fixture:** Executor must not invent callouts merely because the author omitted them. **Malformed fixture:** empty/unknown labels and bad/duplicate ids must be reported even if the current parser renders them uncritically.
- **Combined-file fixture:** malformed, nested, unclosed or duplicated markers must fail the *specified* intake contract; do not represent that an automated validator passed.
- **Metadata fixture:** a no-abstract manuscript with unresolved publication date is editorially usable, but cannot become publishable YAML by guessing a date or abstract.
- **Regression:** No changed runtime code, route-history registry, public content, indexing state, frozen PDF artifacts, production/staging workflow, accepted T25.0/T25.1 records or research repository branches. The existing five-kind parser, citation linker and responsive print styling are not refactored.
