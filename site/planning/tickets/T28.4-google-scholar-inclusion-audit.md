# T28.4 — Google Scholar inclusion, bibliographic and crawler audit

**DRAFT / READ-ONLY / NOT SEALED** · Risk: R2 audit only; remediation risk to be classified separately.
**Scope:** Every production `format: paper` entry (currently six PDF paths in the owner-held manifest), with *The Lab Bubble* essay as **negative control** (must not emit `citation_*`, generate a scholarly PDF or masquerade as a journal paper). The audit must not mark an essay as Scholar-ready merely because Schema.org Article passes.

## Source of truth
Use official [Google Scholar Webmaster Inclusion Guidelines](https://scholar.google.com/intl/en/scholar/inclusion.html), capture section/date/URL. Google Scholar content and indexing rules differ from Google Search Schema.org and Google Search Console: do not substitute Rich Results Test or sitemap submission as proof of Scholar inclusion.

## Required matrix, per paper
1. HTTP GET from canonical **production** article and its distinct PDF, response 200, content-type, no robots/meta/X-Robots block or login/interstitial, stable canonical, accessible HTML full text or **complete author-written abstract**, one paper per URL.
2. Source `format: paper` ⇒ deployed `citation_title` exact visible paper title; exactly the real author(s) in `citation_author`; `citation_publication_date` original **citeable** publication date (not a recent website deploy or v2 upload); `citation_pdf_url` exact absolute same-subdirectory crawlable filename `.pdf`; optional `citation_doi` correct **owner-approved concept DOI**; verify HTML escaping and no contradicted tag/header/PDF claim.
3. PDF has selectable text, file size **at most 5 MB per Scholar guideline**, readable title in large type and actual author below title on page 1, proper References section and parseable full paper; compare visible title/author/date with HTML Highwire; check actual production PDF bytes have expected owner manifest SHA.
4. Browse route: papers reachable by ordinary HTML links from home / research listing, at most roughly ten hops; unique abstract/PDF URL and legacy PDF redirects preserve identity; no unexpected staged noindex. Be careful on multiple Zenodo/website manifestations: do not infer canonical Scholar grouping just from a common DOI.
5. Verify Google Scholar author/paper discoverability and indexed displayed bibliographic record where possible (title/author/year, preferred link and version grouping), but classify unavailable/uncrawlable results **UNVERIFIED**; Scholar indexing is independent of Google Search Indexing and cannot be guaranteed by source compliance. Provide controls for newly published Paper B vs older papers, and note crawl lag.
6. Record high-priority detected differences and source citations; do not rewrite manuscript citations, falsely create venue/journal metadata or add `citation_*` to non-paper essays.
